# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to making participation in Janus Core a harassment-free
experience for everyone.

## Expected behavior

Be respectful, assume good intent, welcome different viewpoints, and focus
criticism on code and ideas rather than people.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, doxxing, and publishing
private information without permission are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through GitHub.
Maintainers may remove content or restrict participation when necessary to
protect the community.
